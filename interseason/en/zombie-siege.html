Zombie Siege

Alliance wave defence: reservation, reinforcements, survival and coordination.

← Home

What is it?

An alliance defence event where zombie waves attack member bases. The goal is not only to survive personally: alliance performance depends on keeping members active and clearing as many waves as possible.

When?

Alliance officers reserve the battle time. An official 2026 update added :15, :30 and :45 reservations in addition to on-the-hour slots. Always follow the server-time slot selected by your R4/R5.

Preparation

Free hospital capacity, heal beforehand, keep troops available and pre-assign strong members to reinforce weaker players. Avoid tying up marches immediately before the event.

How it works

Zombies arrive in successive waves and alliance reinforcements can support threatened bases. Current community documentation describes a standard 20-wave format; the live event screen takes priority if your server shows a variant.

What to do

Reinforce members most likely to fail, report losses quickly and move reinforcements as the threat changes. Strong accounts are most valuable as flexible support.

What to avoid

Do not start with a full hospital or leave weaker members unreinforced. A player knocked out early stops helping alliance progress in later waves.

Rewards

Personal and alliance rewards scale with progress. Exact thresholds can vary by event version, so check the live reward ladder before the start.
