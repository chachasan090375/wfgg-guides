WfGg · LAST WAR

Frontline Breakthrough

Precision mini-game: five stages, buffs, surviving troops and rankings.

← Home

What is it?

A mini-game separate from normal combat: you steer troops across the screen, destroy obstacles/barrels, collect multipliers and weapon boosts, and try to preserve as many soldiers as possible.

When?

2026 guides describe it as a recurring event, commonly on Sunday in current rotations. The event uses 5 stages played in order. Check your live calendar because rotation can change.

Preparation

What you mainly need is time and concentration; hero power is not the main variable. Learn the stage mechanics before burning repeated attempts.

How it works

After clearing a stage you may continue or save progress and return later. Quitting during an active stage attempt counts as a failure. Completing all 5 stages — or failing — triggers final scoring, prioritising stages cleared and then troops remaining as the tiebreaker.

What to do

Prioritise multipliers and weapons that grow the squad efficiently, avoid routes that bleed soldiers, and use early attempts to learn obstacle sequences.

What to avoid

Do not quit mid-attempt. Do not sacrifice a large part of the squad for a secondary pickup when ranking by survivors matters.

Rewards

Current guides say clearing 3 stages unlocks all basic objective rewards, with additional Warzone/elite ranking rewards depending on the edition.
