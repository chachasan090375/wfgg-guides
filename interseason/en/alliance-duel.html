Alliance Duel

Weekly VS competition: requirements, league, chests and battle day

← Home

What is it?

A weekly alliance competition built around daily themes and cumulative scoring.

When?

Commanders above Base level 10 can participate, subject to alliance matchmaking conditions. Alliance Duel technology unlocks with Research Center level 13.

Preparation

Save speedups, recruitment, research and resources for their matching daily themes. Personal chests 4–9 require the related Premium/Super Reward technologies.

How it works

Warm-up matchmaking evaluates overall strength. League grouping also considers alliance strength, historical league performance and promotion/relegation status.

What to do

Plan each day, synchronize major point gains and shield exposed players on Saturday. Saturday battle points are earned through enemy unit casualties under the event rules.

Avoid

Switching alliances during the Duel affects how personal points contribute to alliance scoring. Avoid spending key resources outside the relevant daily theme.

Rewards

Personal chests, alliance rewards and league rewards. Unlock reward technologies before targeting higher chests.
