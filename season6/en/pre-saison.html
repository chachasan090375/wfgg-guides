What to prepare

Season 6 introduces new resources, fishing, SSR → UR hero promotion and a more structured war schedule.

Enchanted Mushroom

Owning it grants the announced +5% Hero Attack bonus. Some fishing upgrades also increase the chance of obtaining a Golden Fish Chest.

War and fishing

City battles are concentrated mainly on Wednesdays and Saturdays. Fishing depends on territory and Fishing Ground level, and loot is exposed outside allied territory.
