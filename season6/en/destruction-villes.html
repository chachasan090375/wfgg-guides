City Destruction — Faction Clash

From week 3 onward, enemy-faction cities are no longer captured: they are destroyed.

Schedule

Level 1 cities open on Wednesday W1, level 2 on Saturday W1 and level 3 on Wednesday W2. From W3 onward, Faction Clash enables the Destroy action against cities of the opposing faction. City actions are limited to Wednesdays and Saturdays.

Permanent destruction

Against an enemy city, the Declare War button is replaced by Destroy. A destroyed enemy city disappears permanently: it is not occupied and cannot be retaken.

Limits and rewards

Declare War and Destroy share a daily limit of 2 actions on the relevant days. Destroyed cities grant Siege Loot Chests; higher-level cities grant more.

Level 1 city2 chests
Level 2 city5 chests
Level 3 city10 chests
💡 Key point: The value is not just the loot: destroying a city permanently changes territorial routes and the pressure on the opposing faction.

Reference source: Cpt Hedgehog's Battle HQ — Season 6. Game content may change; the in-game display takes precedence.
