Final Faction Duel

The Ancestral Temple, three giant cannons and a final battle where every warzone must know its role.

The final week

The Faction Duel fills the last major week of combat: from Monday to Saturday, presidents and vice-presidents choose strategic zones; the battle takes place on Sunday.

Temple and cannons

The central area contains the Ancestral Temple and three Giant Cannons. The cannons speed up capture when they are controlled by the same side capturing the Temple; otherwise they deal damage to the troops inside.

Strategic zones

Each warzone chooses a strategic objective it can attack or support during the battle, in addition to the Ancestral Temple, which remains open to everyone. The #1-ranked warzone in each faction has special freedom and may act on every building.

Victory condition

The first faction to reach 100% capture progress wins the Duel and receives 30,000,000 influence points in the faction ranking.

💡 Key point: The Final Duel is not simply “everyone to the Temple”: split the warzones between the Temple and the cannons, then plan reinforcements in advance.

Reference source: Cpt Hedgehog's Battle HQ — Season 6. Game content may change; the in-game display takes precedence.
