Meteorite Iron War

Off-season cross-server PvP: gather, protect, plunder and know when not to fight.

← Home

What is it?

An off-season event built around a meteorite impact zone. The main goal is to accumulate Meteorite Iron through contaminated-zone presence, gathering and — for players who can defend it — plundering high-value objectives.

When?

There is no sufficiently stable universal schedule; use the event icon and countdown as the source of truth. 2026 guides describe short, very intense contest windows.

Preparation

Stock Advanced Teleports, healing capacity and shields for phases where they are allowed, and decide your risk profile before entering. Mid-power players can score well without forcing every fight.

How it works

The meteorite creates contaminated land with several resource tiers: Debris, Crystals and Stellar Cores. Value and risk increase together. Entering some zones can remove protection, and carrying a Stellar Core naturally attracts plunderers.

What to do

For mid-power accounts, prioritise fast gathering, mobility and preserving score. Strong accounts should plunder only when the gain/risk ratio is favourable. Use recon and teleports to avoid being pinned.

What to avoid

Do not sit still while holding a high-value resource and do not turn every contact into a duel. Surviving with your Meteorite Iron can outscore a lost fight.

Rewards

Meteorite Iron chests, individual/alliance ranks and mixed progression materials. Recent guides list Honor Points, Drone Parts, Chip materials and Hero Shards among possible rewards.
