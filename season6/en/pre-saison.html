Pre-season

The mechanics announced before Season 6 opens and what you should prepare.

By week By topic

Base improvement

The Enchanted Mushroom provides a +5% hero attack bonus when owned. The fishing decoration also increases the chance of receiving an extra Golden Fish Chest.

Initial chance0,5 %
Progression+0.5% per level
Maximum2,5 %

Hero promotion

Braz can be promoted from SSR to UR during Season 6. This progression becomes a major focus of the season.

War organization

Three war periods are planned. City battles are concentrated on Wednesdays and Saturdays, with a safety period to select.

Fishing and grouping

Fishing arrives as a seasonal activity. 4-vs-4 groups are formed automatically by the system, not by leaders.

💡 Key point: Before the season opens, focus on preparing upgrade resources and the heroes who will benefit from Season 6 evolutions.
