⚡ Tesla Awakening

Requirements, Exclusive Weapon and Awakening bonuses.

← Home

Requirements

Tesla must be sufficiently developed, including an Exclusive Weapon at level 20 and the required Awakening Shards.

Skill

His Awakening increases area-damage potential through a chain-lightning effect.

Planning

If Tesla is part of your main squad, prepare his Exclusive Weapon before his Awakening window.
