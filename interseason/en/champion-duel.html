Champion Duel

The post-season PvP tournament where forgetting to re-save your lineup can hurt more than a bad matchup.

← Home

Status

FR/EN/IT/ES names are locked from official app localisations. The format below follows community documentation updated in July 2026 and should always be checked against the active calendar.

Official name · recent community format

What is it?

A bracket-style PvP tournament during the post-season celebration. Combat is virtual: the saved formation fights without consuming your real troops.

When?

The generic 2026 community format describes sign-up Monday–Wednesday, qualifiers on Thursday and the championship Friday–Sunday. Some season celebrations have shifted the opening day, so follow the in-game calendar.

Preparation

Save your actual best formation and re-save/sync it after every hero, gear, research or building upgrade. Account upgrades do not necessarily update the registered Duel formation automatically.

How does it work?

Qualifier: multiple battles, elimination after a loss threshold, then the best commanders advance. Championship: knockout rounds through the final. Rally/base-defense bonuses should not be assumed to apply; combat research remains relevant.

What to do

Sign up even if you are not aiming for the title, re-sync after every power increase and use prediction features when available; recent editions connect predictions to Black Market economy.

What to avoid

Do not leave an old formation registered. Do not assume your current account power is automatically reflected in the Duel. Do not miss pre-match lock windows.

Rewards

Win/qualifier rewards, final-placement rewards and edition-dependent cosmetics. Predictions can also feed Black Market currency.
