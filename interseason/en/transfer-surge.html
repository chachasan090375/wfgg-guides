Transfer Surge

Move Warzones without losing track of account and event progress.

← Home

What is it?

A migration event between similarly aged Warzones. It lets commanders apply to another server in the same transfer group, subject to seat, score and approval rules.

When?

Current community documentation describes a 14-day event: 7 preparation days, 4 application days, 1 transfer day and 2 display days. Exact timing can shift by season, so follow the live countdown.

Preparation

Contact the target alliance before applying, review your transfer status/score and stock the required transfer tickets. Do not submit a “test” application: under current rules, an approved transfer can become mandatory.

How it works

Eligible servers belong to an age-balanced transfer group. The target President handles approvals subject to seats. Individual score uses multiple historical power components. On transfer day you must leave your alliance, not be Warzone President, be on your own Warzone and have all squads home.

What to do

Talk to recruiters before application opens, compare target-server activity and alliances, and verify seats for your status. Record important rankings/progress before migration.

What to avoid

Do not choose a server based only on power rank. Do not apply where no alliance has confirmed a place for you. Approval may become irreversible under current rules.

Rewards

A large amount of progress carries over. Current rules explicitly cover General’s Trial, Doom Walker, Wanted Boss, Zombie Siege, Individual Arms Race, Zombie Invasion, Bullseye Loot, Glittering Market and Total Mobilization, each with its own transfer behavior.
