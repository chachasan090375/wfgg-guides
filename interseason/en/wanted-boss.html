Choosing a squad

Start with the +50% matching troop type, but do not copy a meta lineup blindly. A heavily developed off-type hero can beat an underbuilt theoretical pick. The WfGg Simulator compares your account instead.

Gear

Move your best Gun/Railgun and Data Chips to the main damage dealers, and keep the strongest defensive pieces on the front row. Phase 2 now gives exact transfer suggestions from the gear data you enter.
