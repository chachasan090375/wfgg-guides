Merit Shop

When the shop opens, what carries over, and the order in which to spend your Merit.

Unlock and operation

The shop can be built once the Mushroom Institute reaches level 4. Spending your Merit does not reduce your ranking total.

Dailystock refreshes every 24 h
Weeklyrefreshes every week
Seasontotal limit for the whole season
Unpurchasedunspent stock can carry over

High priorities

The Rainforest Mushrooms from the daily refresh are very useful for seasonal buildings. UR Tactical Card packs, Training Certificates, Weapon Cores and Universal Exclusive-Weapon Shards are also important purchases when they appear.

Secondary purchases

5-minute speedups, extra bait and expensive gifts should generally be bought after the items that directly strengthen your progression.

💡 Key point: Do not empty the shop at random: unspent stock carries over. Save Merit for the stronger unlocks in later weeks.

Reference source: Cpt Hedgehog's Battle HQ — Season 6. Game content may change; the in-game display takes precedence.
