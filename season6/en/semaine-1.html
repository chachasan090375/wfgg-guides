1️⃣ Topic guide

Week 1 — Getting started

Virus Resistance, spores, Merit and first captures: the week that lays all the foundations.

By week By topic

Season resources

Rainforest Mushrooms upgrade Spore Factories. Spores are used to improve Virus Resistance through the Mushroom Institute. War Merit serves as both currency and progression.

Mushroom Institute and Spore Factories

The Mushroom Institute provides Virus Resistance and can be upgraded to level 30. Spore Factories produce spores and require Rainforest Mushrooms to progress.

Protector Field

Keep it at level 10 at the start of the week: this is a priority to avoid wasting resources too early.

Cities and fishing

Day 3: level 1 cities can be captured. Day 6: level 2 cities. Fishing unlocks after conquering a Fishing Zone, and the event starts at 12:00 server time.

Kimberly

Day 4: Kimberly’s Awakening requires a level 20 exclusive weapon and 50 Awakening shards.

💡 Key point: Week 1 is a race for Virus Resistance: upgrade your seasonal buildings in the right order and play as a team.
