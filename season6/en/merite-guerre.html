War Merit

19 personal ranks, permanent bonuses, a daily allowance and end-of-season rewards.

How the ranking works

Total War Merit advances your personal rank. The first 15 ranks use fixed thresholds; the final four are competitive and depend on your position in your warzone ranking.

Top 100Blood Guard
Top 60Centurion
Top 30Legate
Top 10Chief

Important milestones

Ranks provide hero-stat bonuses, Virus Resistance and, at competitive ranks, damage reduction.

Vanguard I58 000 Merit — first shop unlock
Warrior I373,000 — new shop unlock
Elite I546,000 — 10 MR equipment blueprints at season end
Elite III667,000 — new shop unlock

Daily allowance

Each rank grants an allowance to claim every day and once immediately after each promotion. The earlier you rank up, the more resources you accumulate over the whole season.

💡 Key point: For many players, Elite I is an excellent minimum target thanks to the 10 MR equipment blueprints awarded at the end of the season.

Reference source: Cpt Hedgehog's Battle HQ — Season 6. Game content may change; the in-game display takes precedence.
