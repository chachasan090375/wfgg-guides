Alliance Skills

Alliance Energy, authorized roles and six skills that can turn a battle around.

Alliance Energy

Skills consume Alliance Energy, generated notably by donating fishing catches. The cap increases from 350,000 to 700,000 and then 1,400,000 through Faction Technology.

Donation recharge1 donation every 2 h, maximum 24
WarningA catch can only be donated once: choose between faction progression and Alliance Energy.

The 6 skills

Each skill is tied to a specific role.

Frog Donations120,000 — Alliance Leader — 2-day cooldown
Serpent Barrier180,000 — Recruiter — 3-day cooldown
Night Army340,000 — Muse
Serpent Breath360,000 — Warlord
Thunder Feathers420,000 — Steward
Peaceful Return200,000 — Alliance Leader — resets a cooldown

Why it is strategic

The five Altar skills are available only if your alliance controls the corresponding Altar. A skill on cooldown cannot be reused unless Peaceful Return resets it.

💡 Key point: Do not trigger a major skill “on reflex”. Save the energy for a PvP window where its effect can decide the objective.

Reference source: Cpt Hedgehog's Battle HQ — Season 6. Game content may change; the in-game display takes precedence.
