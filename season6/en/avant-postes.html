Warzone Outposts

Irreversible placement, collective construction and permanent destruction: outposts shape the second half of the season.

Placement schedule

Each warzone has 4 slots. A new outpost becomes available every week.

#1Days 21–22
#2Days 28–29
#3Days 35–36
#4Days 42–43

Placement and construction

The president places the outpost in the designated area; its position cannot be changed afterward. Once built, it provides a territorial connection to every alliance in the warzone. The alliance with the highest construction contribution becomes the owner.

Conquest and defense

To attack an enemy outpost, you need a connection through cities/fortresses or a pact with an alliance that has one. If attack progress reaches 100%, the outpost is permanently destroyed and cannot be rebuilt.

💡 Key point: Place outposts with the Sanctuaries already in mind: offensive ones near an enemy Sanctuary, defensive ones to secure your own access routes.

Reference source: Cpt Hedgehog's Battle HQ — Season 6. Game content may change; the in-game display takes precedence.
