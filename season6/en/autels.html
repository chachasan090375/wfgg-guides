The 5 Altars

Which Altars to target, when they open and which choices are mutually exclusive.

Opening schedule

Altar Conquest begins in week 3 and returns every Tuesday at 12:00 server time. Each phase lasts one hour.

Week 3Serpent Altar (lv. 1) + Echo Altar (lv. 2)
Week 4+ Gale Altar (lv. 3)
Week 5+ Feather Altar (lv. 4)
Week 6+ Tree Haven Altar (lv. 5)

Altars and skills

Each Altar unlocks an alliance skill used during faction clashes.

SerpentSerpent Barrier → Fortification
EchoNight Army → Mummy Army Summon
GaleSerpent Breath → Warlord Missile
FeatherThunder Feathers → Tesla Coil
Tree HavenPeaceful Return → Reset

Rules that change your choice

An alliance can hold a maximum of 3 Altars. Two pairs are mutually exclusive: Serpent ↔ Gale and Echo ↔ Feather. Capturing does not require adjacent alliance territory.

💡 Key point: Before Tuesday, decide whether your week will be more defensive or offensive: the mutually exclusive pairs prevent you from taking everything.

Reference source: Cpt Hedgehog's Battle HQ — Season 6. Game content may change; the in-game display takes precedence.
