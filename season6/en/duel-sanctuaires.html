Sanctuary Duel

The major battles of weeks 5, 6 and 7, where the territorial preparation from previous weeks becomes decisive.

When?

The Sanctuary Duel takes place on Saturday in weeks 5, 6 and 7. The scoring window starts at 13:00 server time and ends at midnight.

Who can participate?

Your warzone must either have territory directly connected to the targeted Sanctuary or a connection through an outpost. A pact partner of an eligible alliance may also participate, but that extension does not automatically apply to its entire warzone.

How to capture?

A conquest lasts one hour. You must hold the Sanctuary and fill its capture bar. The surrounding cannons are crucial: if they belong to the same faction as the side capturing, they speed up the capture; otherwise they damage the troops inside the Sanctuary.

💡 Key point: A Sanctuary is often won before Saturday: connections, pacts and outpost survival determine who will actually be allowed to fight.

Reference source: Cpt Hedgehog's Battle HQ — Season 6. Game content may change; the in-game display takes precedence.
