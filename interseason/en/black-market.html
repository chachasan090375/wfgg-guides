Black Market

Treat the post-season shop as a strategic reserve, not a store to empty blindly.

← Home

Status

The Black Market name is locked across FR/EN/IT/ES. Official support confirms the event; the practical mechanics below are supplemented by 2026 community guides.

Official name · mixed official/community mechanics

What is it?

A temporary post-season celebration shop using its own Black Market Cash currency. It returns after seasons and is a major catch-up route for Exclusive Weapon shards.

When?

It opens during the celebration/off-season window. Exact week order depends on season and server. In 2026, the in-game calendar was updated to show Black Market Merchant, Black Market Challenge and Bingo Task when active.

Preparation

Accumulate event currency and decide priorities before spending. WfGg rule: exclusive weapons / scarce progression items first, fillers later. Keep a small reserve if Champion Duel predictions use the same currency in your edition.

How does it work?

The shop presents a small selection of items and supports refreshes. Current guides report that refreshing replaces the visible selection: buy anything you want before refreshing and always claim the free item when offered. Costs, limits and stock vary by edition.

What to do

Take every free item, buy priorities before refreshing, track exactly how many Exclusive Weapon shards you still need and keep currency available while Champion Duel predictions are open.

What to avoid

Do not drain your balance on ordinary speedups/resources while rare targets remain. Do not confuse Black Market with Glittering Market: they use different currencies and serve different goals.

Rewards

2026 community priority: Exclusive Weapon shard choice chests first, then account-dependent bottlenecks such as gear blueprints, chips and other scarce materials. The active shop remains the source of truth.
