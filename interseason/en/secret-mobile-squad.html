Secret Mobile Squad

Daily missions: target useful rewards, refresh wisely and plunder efficiently.

← Home

What is it?

A daily Secret Task system where heroes are dispatched for a set duration and rewards are collected afterward. Mission rarity and score generally indicate reward quality.

When?

Regular tasks reset at daily server reset, while favourites use a separate cycle. Because this is daily content, queue and refresh discipline matters more than one large session.

Preparation

Decide which rewards your account actually needs. Refresh weak unstarted missions before filling queues and keep high-quality tasks. Current guides note that starred/red missions behave differently from ordinary refreshable tasks.

How it works

Missions do not occupy normal march queues. You can also plunder other players’ tasks; current guides describe 5 daily plunder opportunities and a maximum of 3 plunders suffered by one task. Official support states that Golden Mobile Squad increases available mission teams to twice your current number of fleets.

What to do

Refresh before dispatching, share valuable tasks with the alliance and spend plunders on high-quality missions. Keep reasonable free space around your base: official support says overcrowding can prevent tasks from spawning correctly.

What to avoid

Do not instantly launch low-value missions while useful refreshes remain. Do not waste plunder attempts on poor rewards simply to use them.

Rewards

Rewards vary by rarity and task, including resources and progression materials. Optimise value per available mission team.
