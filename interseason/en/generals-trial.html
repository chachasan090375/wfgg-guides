General’s Trial

Solo PvE challenges followed by an alliance elite-force challenge.

← Home

What is it?

A recurring event combining individual progression against Vanguard/Instructor bosses with an alliance stage. Rewards may include Diamonds, Hero EXP, progression materials and shards depending on the current rotation.

When?

Current guides describe a multi-day event, commonly around 3–4 days depending on the version. The documented minimum base level is 8. Follow your server countdown.

Preparation

Choose difficulty carefully: only one difficulty can be selected per edition, and higher tiers unlock after completing the current tier. Prepare your strongest PvE squad.

How it works

You defeat instructors in sequence to open harder challenges. Once enough alliance members complete the required level, R4+ officers can challenge the General’s Elite Forces during their limited availability window.

What to do

Push enough members early so the alliance stage unlocks comfortably. Strong players should help the alliance secure its collective milestone instead of waiting until the final hour.

What to avoid

Do not select an unrealistic difficulty simply for better rewards. Being stuck can cost individual progress and delay the alliance challenge.

Rewards

Individual rewards follow your cleared stages; defeating the elite forces adds alliance rewards. Exact item lists can change with updates.
