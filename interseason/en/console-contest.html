Console Contest

Season 6 off-season competitive mini-games — information dated August 2026

← Home

What is it?

A Season 6 off-season feature built around four Console buildings per warzone. Each machine hosts a mini-game and has its own leaderboard.

When?

Console buildings open from off-season Week 2. Two weekly rounds are currently documented: Monday–Tuesday and Thursday–Friday.

Preparation

Hero power is irrelevant: this is mini-game content. Scout the machine where your score is most competitive before investing time.

How it works

Attempts are unlimited. The best result of the round counts; depending on the game, ranking may reward highest score or the fewest steps/retries.

What to do

Specialize in one or two machines and check the current best before grinding attempts.

Avoid

Do not spread effort across all four leaderboards if rank 1 is your goal.

Rewards

Top 10 per console receive ranking rewards. Current community documentation lists a temporary champion avatar frame and Champion Golden Egg for rank 1.
