Zombie Invasion

Find Zombie Bosses, maximize rewards and coordinate the alliance

← Home

What is it?

A PvE event where Zombie Bosses appear on the map and players fight them. Official support lists Zombie Invasion as a dedicated gameplay topic.

When?

Availability follows the server event calendar. Check the event menu for the active window.

Preparation

Save stamina, prepare your strongest PvE squad and quickly share discovered bosses with your alliance.

How it works

Discoverers and participants earn rewards under the event rules. Some profession skills may interact with invading zombies or bosses depending on their in-game description.

What to do

Coordinate discoveries, avoid duplicate calls and select boss levels appropriate for your group.

Avoid

Do not assume every monster-related profession skill applies to every boss; check the exact in-game effect.

Rewards

Event rewards, resources and progression depend on active objectives and may change between off-seasons.
