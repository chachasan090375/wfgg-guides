Arms Race

The daily schedule that lets the same progression resources pay twice.

← Home

What is it?

An always-on individual competition split into themed phases. Normal account-growth actions are worth much more when completed inside the matching scoring window.

When?

Arms Race runs 24/7 in six 4-hour slots and resets at 00:00 server time. The five themes are Hero Advancement, City Building, Unit Progression, Tech Research and Drone Boost; the order varies and one theme repeats.

Preparation

Save Hero EXP, Skill Medals, build/research/training speedups and drone resources for their matching phase. Whenever possible, overlap spending with the matching Alliance Duel day.

How it works

Each phase scores one family of actions. Current guides describe phase chests that grant badges and a daily goal at 18 badges. Exact phase order changes, so check the Arms Race screen each day instead of trusting a static timetable.

What to do

Land major building/research completions inside their phase, promote troops during Unit Progression and save hero upgrades for Hero Advancement. Look for double-dipping with Alliance Duel.

What to avoid

Do not bleed speedups outside the relevant theme unless the upgrade is genuinely urgent. Never assume yesterday’s slot order will repeat.

Rewards

Phase chests, daily goal rewards and individual ranking rewards. Off-season updates can improve reward contents, so use the live event preview as the final reference.
