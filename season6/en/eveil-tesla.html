Tesla Awakening

Requirements, the Vortex Resonance skill and Awakening bonuses for the third Season 6 hero.

Requirements

Tesla must be at 5 stars, have his exclusive weapon at level 20 or higher, and you need 50 Tesla Awakening shards. His Awakening becomes available in week 5.

Vortex Resonance

The Awakening skill replaces his former fourth skill while retaining its effects. At maximum level, it launches chain lightning that bounces 7 times between enemies. Awakening stars 3 and 5 each add one bounce, bringing the total to 9.

Inductive Current

From 1 Awakening star onward, every bounce applies Inductive Current stacks. This debuff deals continuous energy damage; investing in stars also progressively increases Vortex Resonance damage.

Damage reduction

Awakening also adds damage reduction: 2.5% as soon as Awakening is unlocked, rising to 5% at 5 stars.

💡 Key point: If Tesla is in your main squad, get his exclusive weapon to level 20 before his Awakening week: it is the easiest requirement to prepare in advance.

Reference source: Cpt Hedgehog's Battle HQ — Season 6. Game content may change; the in-game display takes precedence.
