Desert Storm Battlefield

Weekly alliance battlefield: registration, structures, rotations and mistakes to avoid

← Home

What is it?

An alliance-vs-alliance objective battlefield. Each team can register up to 30 players: 20 starters and 10 substitutes. Task Forces A and B have separate matchmaking and results.

When?

Registration opens after Monday server reset. R4/R5 manage participants and battle time.

Preparation

Do not remove gear to manipulate matchmaking: official support states registered players are evaluated using real power. Assign roles, substitutes and entry order beforehand.

How it works

Structures unlock in stages. Field Hospital, Tech Center, Information Center, Arsenal and Mercenary Factory provide tactical effects; Nuclear Silo and Oil Wells become later objectives.

What to do

Play for structures and points, coordinate teleports, and collect plunderable battle points dropped when occupied structures change control.

Avoid

Do not exit expecting to return: once you leave the battlefield, you cannot re-enter.

Rewards

Rewards depend on result and performance. WfGg priority: coordinated attendance, rotations and structure control.
