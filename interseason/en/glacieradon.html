Glacieradon

The Season 2 Nuclear Furnace colossus — not a standard recurring Season 6 off-season boss.

← Home

Scope

Glacieradon is documented as a Season 2 / Polar World mechanic around the Nuclear Furnace reactivation. It is not a Season 6 off-season mechanic by definition. WfGg keeps it in the reference because availability can vary with server age and event rotation.

Community data · season-specific scope

What is it?

A giant boss travelling from the edges of the map toward the Nuclear Furnace. It cannot be attacked while moving; it becomes attackable when it stops to strike a city.

When?

Community documentation places it in Season 2 Week 5. Multiple Glacieradons appear in succession, and both start day and timing have varied across servers.

Preparation

Save stamina, prepare your strongest PvE squad and coordinate with your alliance/warzone. Furnace reactivation and Glacieradon attacks use limited attempts shown in the event interface.

How does it work?

Two objectives run together: reactivate the Nuclear Furnace and eliminate the Glacieradons. A moving Glacieradon is invulnerable. When it attacks a city, commanders can attack it and reinforce the targeted city. Until the Furnace is fully reactivated, Glacieradon can reduce its activation progress.

What to do

Watch Beast Tracks, spend your best attempts only when the boss is attackable, reinforce targeted cities and coordinate Furnace contributions. Check the remaining attempt counter in-game before attacking.

What to avoid

Do not burn attempts with a secondary squad and do not assume a fixed start time: the schedule has changed between servers.

Rewards

Community guides document individual participation rewards and faction/warzone rewards tied to Furnace reactivation and Glacieradon kills.
