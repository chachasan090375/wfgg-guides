Capitol · President & Official Posts

The regular Capitol conquest on your own server.

← Home

What is it?

The Capitol is the final objective of normal server territory progression. Alliances advance through level 1–6 cities toward the center of the map.

Access

To contest the Capitol, an alliance must hold a level 6 city and connected/adjacent territory reaching the Capitol.

Victory

The first alliance to accumulate four hours of Capitol occupation wins. If the event timer expires first, the alliance with the highest cumulative occupation time wins.

President and official posts

The winning alliance leader becomes President and can appoint official positions. These posts provide specialized bonuses for construction, research, training, production, healing and combat stats.

Do not confuse it with

This is your own server’s regular Capitol. Warzone Duel Capitol War is a different cross-server battle with a different capture condition.
