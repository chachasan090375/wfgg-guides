🎣 Complete Fishing Guide

Unlock, bait, mini-game, rewards and best practices.

← Home

Unlock

Conquering a Fishing Ground unlocks the activity. The event opens at 12:00 server time.

Bait

You can use normal or Golden Bait and choose x1 or x3 mode.

Mini-game

Keep the moving bait centered in the gauge to improve reward quality; after the catch, claim the reward quickly.

Progression

Fish can contribute to your Faction, War Merit and different rewards depending on the Fishing Ground and its level.
