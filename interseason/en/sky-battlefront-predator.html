Sky Battlefront & Sky Predator

A warzone-wide week: build the airship, then synchronise rallies.

← Home

What is it?

Sky Battlefront is the event; Sky Predator is the airship built and deployed during it. This is warzone-scale coordination content where organisation matters more than one oversized account.

When?

2026 guides describe a 7-day cycle: donation Monday–Thursday, battle Friday–Saturday and calculation/rewards Sunday. It also recurs during off-season periods. Check your active server version.

Preparation

At the start, the President should deploy the platform quickly. Every member should complete Airship Token tasks, prepare troops and know the rally leaders before battle begins.

How it works

Donations first construct the Sky Predator and then improve its stats during a Rush phase. In battle, the enemy airship cannot be attacked solo; damage is rally-only. Shields appear at HP thresholds and poor coordination can trigger a highly punishing Berserk state.

What to do

Keep donating after construction completes. During battle, use real-time coordination and launch rallies inside a tight window to break shields quickly.

What to avoid

Do not stop donations when construction finishes. Do not send staggered rallies into an active shield. Do not ignore defence either: the enemy airship attacks your warzone.

Rewards

Construction/donation rewards, damage and HP milestones, then ranking rewards. Current guides also describe map chests after destruction that should be collected quickly.
