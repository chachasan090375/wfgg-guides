Warzone Duel

Warzone Duel is a tournament between servers. Depending on bracket size it usually lasts two or three weeks. Monday through Friday, servers score points to earn the Right to Invade.

Saturday: Capitol War

The server holding the Right to Invade attacks. The attacker must seize the enemy Capitol and push capture progress to 100%. If it does not reach 100%, the defender wins.

Four cannons

The Capitol is surrounded by four cannons. Once captured they fire automatically and can greatly speed capture while your side holds the Capitol.

Contaminated Zone

The area around the Capitol heavily slows marching speed and shields cannot be activated there. Pre-positioning and teleport choices matter.

WfGg priority

Kills score points, but buildings decide the war. Capitol and cannons come before individual hunting.
