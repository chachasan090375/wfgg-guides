1️⃣ Week 1 — Getting started

Virus Resistance, Spores, first cities and Kimberly Awakening.

← Home🖼️ Sheets & downloads

Priority: Virus Resistance

Rainforest Mushrooms upgrade Spore Factories; Spores are then used to strengthen Virus Resistance through the Fungus Institute.

Season buildings

The Fungus Institute and Spore Factories should be upgraded together. The Protector Field can remain at level 10 at the very beginning.

First captures

Level 1 and then level 2 cities gradually become capturable. Fishing unlocks after conquering a Fishing Ground.

Kimberly

Her Awakening requires an Exclusive Weapon at level 20 and 50 Awakening Shards.
