What is it?

Recent community documentation describes a four-week cycle that may repeat if the off-season lasts longer. Do not treat it as permanent: server, season and patch can change the order.

Week 1Total MobilizationEnergy Loot Quest
Week 2Bounty HunterHero Growth Battle Pass · Moonstone Blessing
Week 3Glittering MarketOverlord Training Battle Pass · Energy Loot Quest
Week 4Bullseye Loot + Ammo BonanzaDrone Training Pass · Moonstone Blessing

Snapshot: 17/08/2026 — always cross-check the in-game calendar.

Update 2026

Bullseye Loot: by July 2026 the format changed from one 100-level track to 5 rounds of 20 levels. Ammo Bonanza remains the paired ammo-earning event.

Preparation

Conserve the event-specific currencies when they carry over, and only spend after checking the next useful milestone. Do not assume every server receives the same shop contents.
