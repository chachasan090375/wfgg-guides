Age of Oil

A post-Season 2 progression milestone, not a recurring Season 6 off-season event.

← Home

Scope

Age of Oil is introduced after Season 2 and then becomes permanent account progression. It should not be presented as an event that restarts after Season 6. The English name is locked; FR/IT/ES labels remain client-confirmation items.

Well-documented community mechanic · English name locked

What is it?

A research/progression chain that introduces Oil and opens progression beyond HQ 30. It unlocks systems including the Oil Well, a third Tech Center and Drone Parts Workshop as you advance.

When?

It appears during the celebration after Season 2. Current community guides report Tech Center 30 plus at least 40% Special Forces research as the access requirement for the tree.

Preparation

Before it opens, push the Tech Center to the requirement, progress Special Forces and save research/construction speedups. Oil then becomes a major HQ 31–35 bottleneck.

How does it work?

The Age of Oil tree progressively unlocks buildings and research required for HQ expansion. Early priority is Oil production and high-impact speed/research unlocks, then the path required toward HQ Expansion.

What to do

Unlock the Oil Well quickly, improve its output, open the third Tech Center as soon as practical and plan speedups around the research nodes blocking your next HQ.

What to avoid

Do not confuse this post-S2 milestone with Season 6-specific off-season content. Do not spend all Oil on side research before identifying the critical path to your next HQ.

Rewards

The main reward is structural: a new resource, new buildings, more research capacity and access to HQ 31–35. Celebration missions also provide progression chests/rewards depending on edition.
