Ghost Ops

Thursday missions built around required heroes, alliance support and rare shards.

← Home

What is it?

Special Secret Command Post missions similar to Secret Tasks but much more dependent on your hero roster and alliance cooperation. They are especially valuable for named hero shards and other rare progression items.

When?

Every Thursday in documented server-time windows: 00:00–03:00, 06:00–09:00, 12:00–15:00 and 18:00–21:00. Current guides place the unlock on Season 1 Day 60 and require Base Lv. 18+.

Preparation

Before Thursday, review your UR/SSR heroes, star levels and shard needs. Tell the alliance which rare heroes you can lend. Save rewarded assists for UR/UR★ missions with useful special rewards.

How it works

You receive your own missions and can join allies to satisfy hero requirements. Some missions require specific heroes and sometimes a minimum star level. Official support says plundering is limited to servers in the same Apex Arena or Storm Arena group.

What to do

Announce UR★ missions in alliance chat and coordinate requested heroes. Prioritise special rewards tied to hard-to-obtain heroes or exclusive weapons.

What to avoid

Do not burn rewarded assists on weak missions if a valuable UR★ mission is being organised. Distinguish voluntary help from an assist that consumes a rewarded participation.

Rewards

Named hero shards, exclusive-weapon-related items and other materials depending on the mission. Check the special reward before committing heroes.
