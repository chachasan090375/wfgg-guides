← Home🖼️ Sheets & downloads

Altars change the map

An Alliance can control up to three Altars and use the Alliance Skills they unlock during battles.

Snake Altar

It can protect a city in your Faction with a 24-hour durability shield.

Night Army

This skill targets commanders from the enemy Faction within a selected area.

Faction Clash

Battle days focus on connected cities, Altar-unlocked Alliance Skills and Alliance coordination.

Heroes

Braz progresses toward UR, and DVA can be Awakened with an Exclusive Weapon at level 20 and 50 Awakening Shards.
