3️⃣ Topic guide

Week 3 — The Altars arrive

The first Altars change faction strategy, and Faction Clash begins to intensify PvP.

By week By topic

Level 1 and 2 Altars

From Day 2 onward, an alliance can control up to 3 Altars. They do not require an adjacent territorial connection.

Cobra Altar — lv. 1

Usable on cities belonging to your faction. It gives a city a Durability Shield for 24 hours. You can have only one level 1 Altar and one level 3 Altar.

Night Army — lv. 2

This skill attacks enemy commanders in a targeted area; if successful, the targeted commander’s base is destroyed. You can have only one level 2 Altar and one level 4 Altar.

Faction Clash

Days 3 and 6: only connected cities can be attacked, with 2 battles per day. Altar skills and alliance agreements become central.

Braz and DVA

Braz progresses toward UR. DVA can be awakened on Day 4 with a level 20 exclusive weapon and 50 Awakening shards.

💡 Key point: Week 3 marks the start of the real territorial game. Prepare the Altars before battle day, not during it.
