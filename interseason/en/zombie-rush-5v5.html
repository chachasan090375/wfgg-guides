Zombie Rush 5v5

A community-documented 5v5 mode: fast, coordinated and score-driven.

← Home

Confidence level

Zombie Rush 5v5 is documented by recent community calendars/resources, but WfGg has not found a sufficiently detailed official support page as of August 2026. The English name is therefore kept under review and the in-game client remains authoritative.

Community · needs locking

What is it?

A live 5v5 battle where two teams score points by defeating bosses/zombies through a combat sequence. Community sources report a 30,000-point win target.

When?

Recent community calendars place it on a bi-weekly rotation, often on Sunday. This cadence may change; check the event icon and server calendar.

Preparation

Build a five-player team that can actually attend, assign targets and bring your strongest damage squads. As in Winter Storm, coordination often beats one isolated high-power player.

How does it work?

Community sources describe a boss sequence — Baseball, Boxing then Rugby — and a race for score. The core principle is to stop five players from splitting damage across unrelated objectives.

What to do

Enter with a simple plan: common target, instant callouts when the target changes, and save high-impact attacks for decisive moments. Use alliance chat/Discord to announce swaps quickly.

What to avoid

Do not publish a time, boss order or threshold as permanent without checking the live edition in-game: this guide remains community-sourced.

Rewards

Community references associate the mode with Honor/merit-style rewards. Exact chest contents and thresholds should be read from the active event.
