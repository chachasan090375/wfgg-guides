Awakening Exchange

Day 39: transfer Awakening progress between two already-awakened heroes to redirect your investments.

What is exchanged

The event opens on Day 39, in week 6. After a successful exchange, the two heroes swap their Awakening stars, awakened-skill level, equipped gear and named Awakening shards.

Conditions

Both heroes must already have Awakening unlocked and be present in your base, meaning they cannot be out on a march, in a garrison or on an expedition.

Number of exchanges

You start with 3 attempts. One additional attempt is restored every 7 days, up to a maximum of 6. After an exchange, a 300-second cooldown applies before the next one.

Kimberly → DVA/Tesla strategy

Because Kimberly often gets shards more easily, you can push her Awakening first and then transfer that progress to DVA or Tesla, provided the target hero has already unlocked their own Awakening.

💡 Key point: Check the named shards before confirming: they are exchanged too. The preview is your best safeguard.

Reference source: Cpt Hedgehog's Battle HQ — Season 6. Game content may change; the in-game display takes precedence.
