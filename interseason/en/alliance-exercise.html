Alliance Exercise · Marshal’s Guard

Preparation, battle, damage and rewards.

← Home

What is it?

Alliance Exercise is a four-stage alliance event: Selection, Preparation, Battle and Rewards. R4/R5 chooses the difficulty and battle time.

Preparation

Members donate resources to earn Alliance Contribution and raise the alliance damage bonus. Once the damage bonus is capped, donations still grant contribution.

Marshal’s Guard

When battle starts, Marshal’s Guard appears for a 30-minute battle window. Commanders attack through rallies. Rewards are based on total alliance damage and individual damage.

Best practice

Set your strongest squad before battle time, keep enough stamina and coordinate rally leaders. Offline Participation can auto-join your strongest lineup near the end of a rally if space is available.

Rewards

Higher alliance damage improves alliance rewards and unlocks higher difficulties. Higher personal damage improves individual rewards.
