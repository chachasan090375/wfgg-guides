Winter Storm Battlefield

Fast 5v5 battlefield: points, time windows and off-season rewards.

← Home

What is it?

A 5v5 battlefield with ten Commanders per match and a maximum battle time of 10 minutes.

How to win

The first side to reach 30,000 battlefield points wins immediately. If neither side reaches 30,000, the higher score at 10 minutes wins. A perfect tie counts as a defeat for both sides.

Off-season schedule

Official support lists the event on Monday and Saturday during the off-season. Server-time windows: 02:00–04:00, 09:00–11:00 and 19:00–21:00.

Rewards

Possible rewards include Lv.3 Drone Components, training/healing speed-ups and up to 10,000 Honor Points. Full rewards require three total wins during open windows.
