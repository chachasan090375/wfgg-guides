2️⃣ Topic guide

Week 2 — Mobility and diplomacy

Global Expedition, level 3 cities, inter-warzone teleportation, Trading Posts and alliance pacts.

By week By topic

Global Expedition

Zones open on days 1, 3, 5 and 7. Among other rewards, they provide Awakening shards and exclusive-weapon shards.

Day 1Zone 1
Day 3Zone 2
Day 5Zone 3
Day 7Zone 4

City clash

Day 3: level 3 cities become capturable.

Teleportation

You can teleport to other warzones, opening up many more support and positioning options.

Trading Posts

Assignments are made on the first day according to Merit ranking. The event starts on Day 5 and returns every two weeks.

Alliance Pact

A pact requires a shared border. It takes effect after 3 days. If the border disappears, the pact ends. A garrison option becomes available during wars.

Pro Fishing and fishing wars

Pro Fishing rewards the heaviest fish. Fishing Zone wars take place on days 3 and 6, during combat days.

💡 Key point: Week 2 expands the map: think mobility, borders and diplomacy rather than raw power alone.
