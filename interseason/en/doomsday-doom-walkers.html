Doomsday & Doom Walkers

Separate the Doomsday event from Doom Walker enemies and optimise rallies.

← Home

What is it?

Doomsday is the event; Doom Walkers are the enemies that invade the Warzone during that event. The reference therefore keeps two IDs, while this shared guide explains how they fit together.

When?

The documented PvE version is a limited invasion with concentrated Doom Walker spawns. Community rules list Base Lv. 8+ to participate. Note that newer content can also use “Doomsday” in different contexts, so verify the icon and live rules.

Preparation

Keep stamina, march queues and a PvE squad ready. If available Doom Walkers are too strong, wait for the next refresh instead of wasting resources on an unrealistic level.

How it works

The event page lists Doom Walkers and lets you jump to them for rallies. Spawn timing is more concentrated during Doomsday, and server-wide activity can advance collective threat/reward milestones.

What to do

Start or join rallies appropriate to your power, share good targets and use refreshes to find manageable levels.

What to avoid

Do not confuse this PvE invasion with other content carrying the Doomsday name. Do not burn stamina into obviously over-levelled targets when a refresh is near.

Rewards

Kill/rally rewards and collective milestones depend on the edition. Read exact values in the live client.
